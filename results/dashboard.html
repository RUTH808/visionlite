Scope and limitations

One training seed; test accuracy intervals are Wilson binomial 95% intervals, not seed variability. Fashion-MNIST is 28x28 grayscale; performance does not establish real catalog-image performance. Quantization affects Linear layers only; convolutions remain FP32.

Noise evaluation adds Gaussian noise with sigma 0.10 to normalized test images and clips to [0,1]. It is a single controlled stress test. Student models share initialization and data order. Checkpoints are selected on validation accuracy; test data are scored only after training.
